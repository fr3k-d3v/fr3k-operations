# Deputy exit — preservation before termination
Reported issues (CLAIM, unverified): periods of missing data, disappearing historical pay grades and applicants, weak super/ATO integrations and reduced human support.

1. Preserve contract, order form, invoices, renewal date, notice period, SLA, DPA, support correspondence and incident timestamps privately.
2. Request complete machine-readable export including employees, applicants, archived records, pay-rate/classification histories, rostering, attendance, leave, timesheets, approvals and audit logs; document what API/export actually supports.
3. Capture screenshots and correspondence as private evidence, with date, URL, source and hash. Do not publish personal data or confidential contract terms.
4. Compare counts per entity/month, history coverage and cross-module relationships. Flag gaps; request vendor remediation and preservation.
5. Build migration mappings, synthetic dry runs, backup and restore tests.
6. Run parallel payroll periods and independently verify awards, leave and pay calculations against Xero/accountant.
7. Confirm legal retention, staff access and final export; only then consider cutover and contract termination.
8. Contract strategy: written notice and documented service failures, request cure/remedy, data access and negotiated early exit; obtain legal review before asserting termination rights.

Never cancel access before data and evidence are secured. Do not presume a right to terminate without reading actual terms.
