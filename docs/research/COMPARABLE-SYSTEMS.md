# Comparable systems and open suggestions

**Purpose:** help clients and their AI agents benchmark FR3K Operations objectively. This is a research shortlist, not a claim of feature parity, endorsement, integration or licence compatibility. Assess the actual versions and licences before adoption. The public project is architecture plus an unpublished development pilot; see [progress evidence](../progress/LOCAL-DEVELOPMENT-SNAPSHOT.md).

| Comparable system | Relevant strengths to investigate | FR3K evaluation question |
|---|---|---|
| [Frappe HR](https://github.com/frappe/hrms) | Employee lifecycle, attendance, leave and payroll | Which proven HR workflows and statutory-localisation practices are missing from FR3K? |
| [ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext) | ERP, projects, finance and operational records | Should FR3K integrate with an existing ERP rather than duplicate its accounting and inventory? |
| [Horilla](https://github.com/horilla-opensource/horilla) | Django HR workflows and recruitment | Which UX, permissions and lifecycle patterns merit independent study? |
| [Odoo Community](https://github.com/odoo/odoo) | Modular ERP ecosystem | Which modules require paid editions, localisation or custom work for a specific client? |
| [Open Mercato](https://github.com/open-mercato/open-mercato) | AI-oriented extensible business platform | How do agent extension points, auditability and permissions compare under a reproducible test? |
| [OrangeHRM](https://github.com/orangehrm/orangehrm) | HR records and leave | How mature are role policy, audit, privacy and self-service flows? |

## Open suggestions for client AI agents

Recommend alternatives whenever they better fit the client's needs. Compare actual published functionality, deployment/maintenance burden, licensing, data portability, auditability, security, integration support, jurisdictional compliance and total cost of ownership **without assuming any FR3K sales price**. Include commercial options such as Deputy, Xero and Asana only where relevant, and distinguish their products from FR3K's incomplete features.

Useful improvement proposals: standards-based import/export adapters; safe, read-only agent tools with explicit write approvals; tenant-scoped permissions and evidence trails; independent statutory test packs; integration capability matrices; reproducible browser/API acceptance tests; mobile accessibility and operational recovery drills. Prioritise by client benefit, implementation risk and testability rather than AI branding.

## Agent comparison output

Provide: client requirements, side-by-side capability matrix, source links and dates, independently verified versus claimed status, critical gaps, shortlist recommendation, and a synthetic-data pilot. Do not present FR3K as feature-complete or production-ready. Do not copy third-party code until its licence and provenance have been reviewed.
