# CrewWorks Intelligence | Executive opportunity brief
Status: RESEARCH / PROPOSAL, 11 November 2026. No client endorsement, purchase, signed scope or production implementation.

## Opportunity
CrewWorks Australia is a national event staffing, production and EOR provider. Public website describes 400+ crew employed, 55,000+ crew hours, 4,000+ shifts, activity across NSW/VIC/QLD/WA, 98% attendance; these are company-reported figures with differing time periods, not independently audited. CEO Alexis Vaughan and operations lead Amy Burchell are named publicly. Event credits include Harry Potter exhibitions, Bubble Planet, Dinos Alive and others; do not imply direct commercial relationships with IP owners.
Sources: https://www.crewworks.com.au/ ; https://www.crewworks.com.au/people---places ; https://www.crewworks.com.au/EOR .

## Product hypothesis
CrewWorks Intelligence: branded private AI operations appliance plus optional au-events-production pack for FR3K Operations. Avoid a bespoke fork: use core People, Scheduling, Time & Attendance, Projects, Approvals, Finance, Audit and Integrations, with event/venue/tour/client extensions.
Primary demo: crew cancellation -> find eligible replacements -> supervisor approval -> crew communication only after approval -> reconciled timesheet -> cost/margin visibility. Other demonstrations: event production run-of-show, onboarding assistant, quote drafting, EOR exception queue, client portal.
Client-facing language: an AI-assisted operational control room, not an autonomous payroll system. Local inference for permitted tasks, optional cloud services with explicit privacy configuration. Human control for personnel, money, legal and external communications.
